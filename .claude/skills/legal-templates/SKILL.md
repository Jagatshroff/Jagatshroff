# Legal Templates

Attorney-drafted startup/tech legal templates (`General-Legal/legal-templates`), released
under CC0 1.0. Provided as LLM-optimized Markdown and original .docx; highlighted text marks
fields to customize.

## Where it lives
Cloned at `~/tools/legal-templates` (`templates/`, one directory per template).

## How to use
- Pick the template under `templates/`, fill the highlighted fields, and adapt to the user's
  facts/jurisdiction. The repo asks (not legally required) that you keep the General Legal
  credit footnote.

## Important
These are starting points, **not legal advice**. Recommend review by a qualified attorney for
the user's jurisdiction before use or signing.

## When to use
Drafting a first version of a common startup legal document.
