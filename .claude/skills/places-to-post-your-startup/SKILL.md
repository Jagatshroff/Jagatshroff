# Places To Post Your Startup

Curated list (`mmccaff/PlacesToPostYourStartup`) of subreddits and websites where founders
can post a startup for visibility, feedback, and early users.

## Where it lives
Cloned at `~/tools/PlacesToPostYourStartup` (Markdown lists: Reddit + Websites, alphabetized).

## How to use
- Pull the relevant entries (subreddits like r/alphaandbetausers, r/buildinpublic; sites and
  directories) and recommend fitting venues for the user's launch.
- Pair with the `directory-submissions` / `launch` marketing skills for a full launch plan.

## When to use
Finding where to announce/launch a startup or get early users. It is a reference list, not an app.
